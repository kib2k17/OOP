# Functional Requirements

FR-01 Authenticate users.
FR-02 Enforce role-based permissions.
FR-03 Create/read/update/deactivate operators.
FR-04 Manage departments.
FR-05 Manage POCT devices.
FR-06 Manage test methods.
FR-07 Record operator competency.
FR-08 Automatically classify competency as Current, Due Soon, or Expired.
FR-09 Record QC and automatically determine Pass/Fail.
FR-10 Record corrective action for failed QC.
FR-11 Track device maintenance and due status.
FR-12 Track inventory by item, lot, quantity, and expiration.
FR-13 Detect low stock, expiring, and expired inventory.
FR-14 Display a management dashboard.
FR-15 Provide administrative reports.
FR-16 Maintain an audit log.
FR-17 Persist data in PostgreSQL.
FR-18 Support responsive browser-based use.
